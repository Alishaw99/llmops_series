An AI Agent is an intelligent software system capable of understanding goals, reasoning about problems, making decisions, using external tools, and executing tasks with varying degrees of autonomy.
Unlike traditional AI applications that primarily respond to individual prompts, AI agents can carry out multistep workflows, interact with external environments, and adapt their actions based on feedback.
For example, a traditional chatbot might explain how to analyze a dataset. An AI agent could retrieve the dataset, validate its quality, execute Python code, generate visualizations, interpret results, and prepare a report.
